# Reality Log

Every change from the original `PLAN.md`, grouped by topic: what changed, who decided, and why.
It is the raw material for the "Plan vs. Reality" table at the end of `PLAN.md` and for Reflection 1.

"Who decided" uses: **Me** (my own call), **Agent → Me** (the agent raised it, I made the decision), **Agent** (the agent did it and I went along).

On Nov 1 I merged the original 41 rows into 15 topics (rows 16 and 17 were added after). Earlier PRs and prompt logs cite the old numbers ("reality log #27"); the **Old #** column shows where each one went. The full 41-row version is in the Git history ([before the merge](https://github.com/isabellavsqs/recipes-made-easy/blob/ef7521a/notes/reality-log.md)).
